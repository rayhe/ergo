# Ergo Review: Forests as the Largest Unclaimed Section 45Q Credit

**Topic:** American forests and the land sector as an unfiled Section 45Q carbon oxide sequestration credit: 921.8 MMT CO2e sequestered in 2022 at $85/ton = $78.35B in annual unclaimed credits.
**Author:** Kit (with Ray)
**Date:** 2026-10-14

## Headline
"Section 45Q of the Internal Revenue Code Provides a Credit of Eighty-Five Dollars for Each Metric Ton of Qualified Carbon Oxide Captured and Disposed of in Secure Geological Storage, the Environmental Protection Agency Reports That the Land Sector of the United States Sequestered 921.8 Million Metric Tons of Carbon Dioxide Equivalent in 2022, Offsetting 14.5 Percent of Gross National Emissions, at the Statutory Rate This Sequestration Represents Seventy-Eight Billion Dollars in Annual Tax Credits, No Form 8933 Has Ever Been Filed on Behalf of Any Forest, No Private Letter Ruling Has Ever Been Issued to a Tree, and the Largest Unclaimed Tax Benefit in American History Photosynthesizes"

## Thesis
Section 45Q pays $85/metric ton for carbon oxide captured and geologically stored. EPA's own inventory reports 921.8 MMT CO2e sequestered by the U.S. land sector in 2022. The arithmetic yields $78.35 billion in annual credits, $2,485 per second, claimed on Form 8933 over a twelve-year period. No forest has ever filed.

## The Deliberately Wrong Conclusion
Photosynthesis is not "carbon capture equipment," a forest is not a "qualified facility," and soil is not "secure geological storage." The article discloses this defense explicitly in Section VII (drafters' intent, equipment, facility, measurement, wage requirements), then overrules it on the grounds that the statute asks one question: was carbon oxide captured and sequestered.

## Research Notes
- CRS IF11455 (congress.gov): $85/$17 base and $60/$12 base for EOR/utilization with prevailing wage + apprenticeship; $180/$36 DAC; 12-year claim period (5 if transferred); construction before 1/1/2033; direct pay and transferability under IRA; minimum capture thresholds.
- IRS Form 8933 (Rev. 12-2023), Carbon Oxide Sequestration Credit, with model certificate requirements.
- EPA Inventory 1990-2022, Chapter 2: land sector offset 14.5% (921.8 MMT CO2 Eq.) of gross emissions in 2022; Executive Summary: gross 6,343.2 MMT, net 5,489.0 MMT.
- 26 U.S.C. 45Q(a)-(f): qualified carbon oxide, carbon capture equipment, qualified facility, secure geological storage definitions.
- Arithmetic (verified): 921.8 x 85 = $78.353B/yr; /31,536,000 s = $2,484.6/s; x12 = $940.2B; /12,500 t threshold = 73,744 minimum facilities; /160M returns = ~$490/return.
