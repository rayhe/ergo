# 7-Critic Panel Evaluation: "The Sun as an Unlicensed Nuclear Facility"

## 1. General Editor — 9.0/10
The article follows the established Ergo format precisely: absurd-yet-rigorous single-sentence headline, scholarly deck, nine Roman-numeral sections building a systematic case from facility to licensing regime to capacity comparison to effluent to recordable event to penalty to liability gap to remedy to conclusion, genuine footnotes with a Sources section, pullquotes at the load-bearing beats, and the deadpan "Ergo." close. The escalation is strong: the facility description, the exhaustive §101 verb list, the 3.8-quadrillion capacity ratio, the heliosphere exclusion area, the Carrington LER that was never filed, the $9.8 billion accrued penalty, the judgment-proof star, and the scram-order remedy. Minor weakness: Section IV (effluent) leans on the same "no enforcement action" beat as the rain piece's permit gap, and the penalty section's "decorum" line is the one place the voice nudges toward the reader. During editing, the headline's "four quadrillionths" was corrected to "less than one quadrillionth" after the actual ratio (2.6×10^-16) was computed, and the day count was recomputed exactly (26,342 days, $9,805,546,080).

## 5. Legal Accuracy — 9.0/10
The Atomic Energy Act citations are accurate: §101's unlawful-without-license prohibition with its exhaustive verb list, §50.10's construction-permit requirement, §103's commercial-license authority with the 40-year maximum term (verified via the §103(c) amendment text). The §234 penalty mechanism and the FY2025 $372,240 figure are correct, and the continuing-violation per-day rule is quoted from the rule itself. The Price-Anderson framing is legally sound in its setup: the Act limits the liability of the Commission's reactor licensees and indemnifies them, currently up to $15.5 billion per incident per CRS. The article's deliberately wrong conclusion, that the Sun is a "utilization facility" subject to licensing, is the genre's disclosed conceit, stated outright in Section II, and the piece never pretends the statute defines stars as facilities; it openly notes the statute "does not say except stars." Passes threshold, with the standard Ergo deduction for creative inference from statutory text.

## 6. Research Rigor — 9.0/10

Transparent throughout.
