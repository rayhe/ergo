# 7-Critic Panel Evaluation: "Starlings as an Unwaivered Aerobatic Display"

## 1. General Editor — 9.0/10
The article follows the Ergo canon precisely: long factual headline, scholarly deck, Roman-numeral sections building a systematic case from rule to waiver to display to formation to the six prohibitions in order to parachutes to quantification to founding flight to objection to conclusion, genuine footnotes with a Sources section, two pullquotes, and the deadpan "Ergo." close. The arc escalates cleanly: the six prohibitions, the waiver system that exists to excuse them, the seven-neighbor mechanism that replaces a flight lead, the 500-billion-arrangement arithmetic, the paragraph-by-paragraph checklist, the parachute file, the million-bird scale, the 100-bird founding release, the honestly stated objection, the conclusion. The load-bearing beam is the Section IV maneuver, that Section 91.111(b) requires an arrangement with the pilot in command of each aircraft in the formation and a million-bird formation therefore requires roughly 500 billion arrangements, and it holds through all ten sections. Minor weakness: the Section V concessions on paragraphs (c) and (d) are honest but slow the checklist's momentum.

## 2. Voice Coach — 9.0/10
Deadpan investigative register throughout, matching the crows and deer pieces, with the humor emerging exclusively from bureaucratic precision rather than jokes. Standout lines: "The display does not check. That is the point," "The flock is a lookout with a million eyes and no logbook," "To comply, the birds would have to perform above 1,500 feet and then commute down without maneuvering, which is not a display at all. It is a commute," and "One of these systems moves a million performers a night. The other one writes the rules." The piece never winks; the closest it comes is "commuting with style," which lands as straight-faced procedural suspicion. Zero em dashes in the draft, well within house discipline. Excellent voice match.

## 3. Ethics — 9.0/10
All regulations and event facts cited are real and quoted accurately where quoted: the Section 91.303 prohibitions and definition, the Section 91.111 formation and collision rules, the Section 91.113 see-and-avoid rule, the Section 91.905 waiver list, the Form 7711-2 waiver and briefing procedure, the Ballerini 2008 seven-neighbor finding, the Sort Sol scale, the Somerset and Brighton counts, and the Schieffelin release figures. The deliberately wrong conclusion, that starlings violate Part 91, is the disclosed conceit, and the piece is unusually honest about the limits of its own evidence: the Shakespeare motive is reported and then declined as uncorroborated, the paragraph (c) and (d) concessions are stated in full, the visibility claim is explicitly limited to the absence of any measurement rather than a measured figure, and Section IX states the not-an-aircraft, normal-flight, and European-jurisdiction objections in full before declining them. No ethical concerns.

## 4. Social/Shareability — 9.0/10
"One million birds perform an unwaivered airshow every night at dusk" is immediately graspable and funny at the headline level alone, and the hero image, an inspector with a blank waiver form staring up at a swirling murmuration over a small town, is built for the thumbnail. The most shareable paragraphs are the 500-billion-arrangement arithmetic, the 500-birds-per-cubic-meter roost density, and the founding flock of 100 birds becoming 200 million. The late-October season opening gives it a news-adjacent timeliness the purely natural-history pieces lack. High share potential.

## 5. Legal Accuracy — 8.5/10
The citations are accurate: 14 CFR 91.303(a) through (f) and its aerobatic-flight definition, 91.111(a) and (b), 91.113(b), 91.307(c) as summarized by AOPA, 91.905, and the FAA waiver procedures for Form 7711-2. The piece correctly states the waiver mechanism, including the holder's duty to brief every participant, and correctly notes that the waiver list includes all three rules the display implicates. The deliberately wrong conclusion, that the starling display violates Part 91, is the genre's disclosed conceit, and Section IX states the escape hatch explicitly. Passes threshold, with the standard Ergo deduction for creative inference from regulatory text, here the application of "aircraft" and "pilot in command" to birds, which the piece labels as author's application in the footnotes and confronts in the objections.

## 6. Research Rigor — 8.5/10
Strong sourcing across independent authorities: the eCFR and Cornell LII text for the regulations, the FAA's own waiver procedures, the peer-reviewed Ballerini 2008 PNAS paper for the coordination mechanism, and multiple independent guides and ornithology sources for the display counts. Competing characterizations are handled carefully: the peer-reviewed seven-neighbor rule is kept distinct from the author's arrangement arithmetic, the peak 200-million population is kept distinct from the recent 85-to-93-million estimate, and the Shakespeare legend is reported as uncorroborated rather than repeated as fact. Minor weakness: the Sort Sol scale rests partly on a general encyclopedia entry rather than a primary Danish survey, and the display-duration figures rest on a photography guide rather than a peer-reviewed source, though both claims are uncontroversial and corroborated by independent guides. The steady-speed model finding was included rather than suppressed, which is the right call.

## 7. Data Presentation — 9.0/10
Numbers are presented with consistent precision and always sourced: 1,500 feet, three statute miles, four nautical miles, 60 degrees of bank, 30 degrees of pitch, seven neighbors, 6.5 on average, 0.7 to 1.5 meters spacing, 2,600 birds in the Rome study, 23 percent of 328 falcon hunts, up to one million birds at Tonder, 500 billion pairwise arrangements, 3.5 million neighbor arrangements, more than 500 birds per cubic meter, 100,000-plus at Somerset, 40,000 at Brighton, 60 plus 40 founding birds, 200 million at peak, 85 to 93 million recently. The comparison framework, a licensed airshow versus a nightly million-bird display with no waiver, no briefing, and no crowd line, is devastating and verifiable. Clear, consistent, sourced.
