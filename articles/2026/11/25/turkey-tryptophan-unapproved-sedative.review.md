## 5. Legal Accuracy — 9.0/10
The FDCA citations are accurate: 321(g)(1) quoted from the Code with all four clauses, 321(p) new-drug definition, 355(a) approval requirement, 331(d) prohibited act as recited in the agency's own 2025 warning letters, and the 333(a) penalty tiers (misdemeanor up to one year and $1,000; second offense felony up to three years and $10,000). The intended-use doctrine is stated correctly as the test the agency applies, and the article concedes the doctrine's limit: the statute says "other than food," and no court has held a holiday dinner to be a drug. One standard Ergo deduction: the core inference (a turkey served for sleep is "administered" rather than "consumed as food") is the creative leap the genre requires, and the piece discloses the counter-authority rather than hiding it.
